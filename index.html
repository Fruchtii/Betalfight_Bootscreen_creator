    How to upload it to your quad
    
    
      
  1. Download the BMP. It's the safest format, because it has no alpha channel.
    2. 
      
  2. In Betaflight Configurator, open the OSD tab, then Font Manager.
    3. 
      
  3. Select your font preset, or open your own .mcm font file.
    4. 
      
  4. Click "Select custom logo image", choose the BMP and check that both green ticks appear.
    5. 
      
  5. Click "Upload Font". The logo is stored inside the font, so it's uploaded together with it.
    6. 
    

    
Configurator only accepts pixels that are exactly black, white or green (#00FF00, which becomes transparent). Any other color fails the color check, and so does real transparency, because Configurator ignores the alpha channel. That's why this tool never writes alpha.

    
This works for analog (MAX7456) OSDs. Digital systems like HDZero, Walksnail and DJI/WTFOS use the goggles' own font instead.
